Lo que dijiste
Last quarter, we were about to release a new version of our RISC score and tool to production. I was the QA automation engineer and I was responsible for the final regulation. During the last tests, I found a critical bug. The release reset every user's RISC score to zero, so people would have lost all their configurations data. I was in the go-no-go meeting with the Dutch engineering leads and the CTO, and I told them we had just stopped the release. I showed them the filing test and the exact steps to reproduce it. I said that staying quiet would have been much more expensive than a one-day redilay. They agreed, and we postponed the release. The developers fixed the bug the next day, I rerun the full regression, and we reset safely. As a result, we avoid data loss for all our users, and the team adds a new check to the pipeline to catch the kind of issue earlier.
