Lo que dijiste
Well, when I see a flaky test, the first thing I want to do is understand why it's the origin from this flaky test. Why someone thinks that this test was a good idea. So, this is the first thing, I understand why it exists or why it's a deprecated test. Maybe in other times the test was a good idea, but I don't know, for change in the application or someone or something else, the test has become deprecated. So this is the first thing. Then, I understand that if exist this flaky test, it needs a new test that cover all the other characteristics that this flaky test is not doing. and I try to create or update the test, it depends if it's necessary, or it's directly eliminated.
